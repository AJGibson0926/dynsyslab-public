# Miscellaneous computes inventory and audit

The side-car covers three different experiments that can produce a convincing phase portrait. A measured-window decode uses fresh observations at every sample. A state readout learns physical labels from such measurements. A free forecast integrates a learned field from one initial window. The tools preserve those distinctions in their model, curve and score records. [Runnable guide](TOOLKIT.md)

## Computational inventory

| Operation | Implementation and reusable result |
|---|---|
| Prepare observations | [Sampling](sampling.py) handles increasing clocks, causal fractional taps, time weights, speed-based thinning and weak-window operators. [Tapped history](tapped_history.py) interpolates a value/rate pair from consistent value/rate/acceleration labels. [Prehistory](prehistory.py) accepts a known reversible flow and checks a backward-generated startup by independent forward integration. The native `integral` command calls the existing exact-on-piecewise-linear finite-window integral service. |
| Learn and apply a delay basis | [History](history.py) and the [native Hankel kernel](hankel.hpp) accumulate multi-record, multichannel Gram matrices without assembling a dense trajectory Hankel. Project values/rates, freeze or extend a signed prefix, recover the historical scalar normalization, and compare two visits. [Modal charts](modal.py) carry the tap layout, projection, physical decoding and working-coordinate gain for full or truncated physical-delay layouts. |
| Fit a sparse vector field or general map | [Dictionaries](features.py), [regression](regression.py), [field fitting](models.py) and [sparse STLSQ](sparse_stlsq.py) support independent records, normalization and sparse per-output fits. Use polynomial, globally bounded, regularized reciprocal, local, energy-gated or harmonic functions. Sampling weights and finite-duration weak regression handle strongly nonuniform contributions. [General map regression](structured.py) accepts different input and output dimensions, including physical-state-to-observation-rate fits. |
| Fit local and oscillatory fields | [Compact radial dictionaries](compact_field.py) support an affine contribution and sparse local residual, for scalar or vector outputs. [Local tangent fields](local_field.py) use quadratic fits on two-dimensional local charts and a [native evaluator](local_rhs.hpp). [Paired fields](paired.py) fit amplitude and angular rates with optional harmonic locking. These expose assumptions a user can vary across candidates. |
| Fit laws with known structure | [Structured maps](structured.py) implement imposed kinematic rows, residual learning against an explicit prior, pair sharing by physical lag, and damped interior taps with optional endpoint feedback. [Filtered waveform fitting](waveform_fit.py) accounts for each record's initial transient. [Calibration](calibration.py) optionally adjusts coefficients on an existing sparse support against training rollouts. |
| Freeze a model and inspect its equations | [Model container](modal.py), [field codec](field_codec.py), [equation export](equations.py) and [historical RPE adapter](compat_rpe.py) retain the signed chart and field together, including nested endpoint laws. Export explicit affine matrices or nonlinear polynomial rows, or the complete composite evaluator. Historical format conversions are isolated from fresh fits. |
| Run an independent forecast | [Replay](replay.py) accepts one window or one signed state. [Integration](integration.py) provides independent exact affine and compiled polynomial paths alongside adaptive integration. The [RK4 callback](rollout_kernel.hpp) supports arbitrary dimension, irregular output times, a step budget and magnitude/nonfinite guards. Its [polynomial evaluator](polynomial_kernel.hpp) compiles active terms once. |
| Reconstruct state and compare receivers | [Readouts](readouts.py) provide inverse-distance neighbor estimates and a smooth derivative-consistent scalar readout. [Reconstruction](reconstruction.py) compares channel, delay and scaling designs over their common usable clock. [Discrete delay models](delay_dmd.py) fit a within-record snapshot advance and a separate affine readout. The [receiver companion](../receiver_reconstruction/README.md) generates retarded monopole and correction terms, reports neighborhood rows/weights, and scores per ring. |
| Diagnose a forecast or crossing | [Diagnostics](diagnostics.py) reports physical and relative RMS, actual time/sample coverage, peak timing and height, and decoded versus differentiated rates. [Controls](controls.py) and [audit](audit.py) compare restarts, tolerances, readouts and visits. [Delay consistency](tapped_history.py) tests whether an evolved tap reproduces the stated lag of its own output. [Native intersections](crossing_kernel.hpp), [continuous refinement](crossings.py) and [witness matching/stencils](witness_checks.py) support independent solver and resolution checks. |
| Run, compare and display studies | [Fixed-basis rank sweep](study.py), [fixed-basis CLI](run_study.py), [physical-lag study](modal_study.py), [split/publication helpers](manifest.py) and [toolkit CLI](toolkit.py) cover model selection and export. [Comparison pages](comparison.py) build independently clocked overlays, reference-scale and model-scale pages. [Viewer adapter](viewer.py) uses the existing native Viewer. [Trajectory resolution](../trajectory_resolution/README.md) handles geometrically bounded vertices, clock/interpolation error and unit labels. |

## Reading the four-coordinate RPE plot

For the selected 5 mm echo figure, simulated RPE trajectories provided pressure, rate and acceleration labels. Two taps of pressure and rate gave four raw coordinates and hence a full invertible four-mode chart. The two pressure-derivative rows used the known identity that the derivative of pressure is its rate. Sparse regression fitted the two acceleration rows on nine simulated training trajectories using the earlier waveform-calibrated scalar echo law as a prior and fitting a correction. Mapping that field into the signed chart gave the autonomous modal law. The gold curve decodes a measured window at every sample. The dashed blue curve integrates the fitted law from one window. The later 6-, 8-, 10- and 12-coordinate **direct** fits instead regressed all modal derivative rows from scratch. These are different model specifications even when plotted on identical axes.

## Verification and limits

The original four-mode candidate ranking weighted validation trajectories 17 and 3 at 60% and 20% and the displayed training trajectory 0 at 20%. Trajectory 4, starting at radius 2.5, was held out of that fit and ranking. Its initial radius falls inside the training range. The current generic study selects candidates using disjoint validation records. This difference matters when comparing a new study's scores to the original figure.

On the common five-ring held-out clock, the four-mode baseline produced five pressure peaks. Its relative RMS errors were 38.3% for echo and 93.4% for rate. The direct 6–12 fits had held-out echo errors of 101–102.5% and rate errors of 100.7–102.0%. Close phase-curve shape can coexist with substantial time-aligned error. Pulse timing, peak height, waveform error and rollout coverage are available separately.

- The standalone suite includes mathematical and CLI tests and the receiver and resolution companion tests. Coverage includes arbitrary observation dimension, fractional delay interpolation, locked signs, weak moments on irregular clocks, sparse refits, portable and structured fields, signed equation export, failed/partial integrations, full reference retention, crossing refinement, split integrity and readouts. [Computation tests](test_computations.py) · [CLI tests](test_toolkit_cli.py) · [Basis and control tests](test_sidecar.py)
- For the selected **direct** RPE fits, rebuilding from the archived training coordinates and their derivative labels recovered identical supports with 24, 48, 78 and 104 active coefficients. The largest relative coefficient change was below 7.89 × 10⁻¹⁶. On both published cases at each order, exact affine propagation agreed with the archived decoded curves to relative trace error below 4.81 × 10⁻¹¹. Compiled RK4 at 0.00005 τ agreed within 2.73 × 10⁻⁸. The clock-aligned errors and detected peak counts also reproduced. These figures measure agreement with the archived models. Their physical errors against RPE are separate outputs. [Parity results](examples/archived-parity.json)
- For each of the five **structured** deployments with 4 through 12 coordinates, its signed-state derivative and decoded measurements were checked against the independent historical evaluator at 392 states on each of the two trajectories. The maximum observed relative derivative difference against that evaluator was zero. A separate comparison with the original four-mode figure code agreed within 1.84 × 10⁻¹³ on the signed derivative and also checked the displayed echo and rate. Verification of this family covered the saved fields and decoders. The original hyperparameter searches and coefficient fitting sequence were not repeated. [Optional archive verifier](examples/verify_archived.py)
- The [synthetic fixture](examples/make_demo.py) exercises the public study, equation export, reconstruction and native Viewer routes on a separate system. In this constructed six-dimensional harmonic example, the held-out value/rate relative RMS was about 0.323 / 0.497 with two taps and below one part in a million in both channels with three taps. Performance on this fixture is a check of known dynamics. For another observable or system, use independent records and inspect derivative error, free rollout, coverage and physical consistency.
