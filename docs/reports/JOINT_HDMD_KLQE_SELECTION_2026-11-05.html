Joint Hankel DMD → subset selection → echo-only KLQE

The infrastructure is implemented, but this experiment did not produce an accurate state estimator. The requested large bank contains 3,188 approximate modal coordinates. It reconstructs its fitted snapshots extremely well, yet its important individual coordinates have poor autonomous propagation and enormous cancelling reconstruction contributions. Tested subsets fail. A separately fitted 80-mode model follows clean echo closely while estimating the radius incorrectly. These results support your requirement to balance reconstruction and observability, and show why dynamical accuracy must be a third requirement.

1. What a useful subset must do

Your proposed two roles are correct: retained coordinates must reconstruct the desired state, and echo measurements must reveal those coordinates accurately enough. Raw column norms of Cx are insufficient: multiplying ψⱼ by 100 and dividing its C column by 100 changes that norm without changing the model. Individual modal contributions also cancel and correlate. A ranking must therefore evaluate whole subsets after a declared normalization.

For a proposed conjugate-complete subset S, convert its coordinates to independent real coordinates z and normalize each retained complex mode by its training RMS sⱼ. Transform both C maps consistently. State errors are divided by chosen output accuracy scales Dx; this experiment uses training standard deviations, approximately 0.31554 for radius and 1.86160 for velocity. Sensor noise is σy=0.0382082, one percent of the echo standard deviation at fitted sample times.

z = D−1ΨS,   F = Dx−1Cx,SD,   H = Cy,SD / σy
Φ = exp(ASΔt),   Wo = Σk=0,…,N−1 (Φk)ᵀ HᵀH Φk
P0|Y = (I + Wo)−1,   Jnoise = tr[F exp(AST) P0|Y exp(AST)ᵀ Fᵀ] / dim(x).

This is a state-weighted uncertainty criterion: uncertainty in a weakly observed modal direction is costly when that direction strongly affects x. The unit prior applies after modal RMS normalization; independent measurement noise and exact linear dynamics are assumptions. The benchmark conditions on N observations at 0,…,(N−1)Δt and predicts x at T=NΔt. It is not the covariance of the actual mismatched KLQE. A plain pseudoinverse of Wo would incorrectly give zero penalty to completely unseen directions; the prior prevents that.

The native assessment also returns:

  • State truncation error: average squared error of Dx−1(x−Cx,SΨS), averaged over samples and state components.
  • Echo truncation error: summed squared error of y−Cy,SΨS, divided by total y energy.
  • Autonomous propagation error: mean |ψⱼ(tₖ₊₁)−exp(λⱼΔtₖ)ψⱼ(tₖ)|²/sⱼ². Actual sample gaps are used.
  • Gramian spectrum and directions: weakest visible directions, numerical rank, and conditioning. Singular directions generally mix eigenfunctions; they are not themselves an index list of modes to retain.

For this first search, the scalar score is the sum of all four MSE terms with weights one. These weights are explicit and provisional. The component table should guide a Pareto comparison before choosing weights or imposing application-specific error limits. A low scalar score is not automatically acceptable; all candidates can be bad.

No C coefficients are refitted after selection. The retained columns are preserved, apart from an equivalent conjugate reduction and RMS normalization. Refitting C after removing modes would be a distinct experiment and could conceal loss of essential contributions.

2. Exact data and computation pathway

The unforced viscous RPE trajectory is the previously displayed high-resolution ring-down: IC (r,v)=(2.333386598601735,0), τ=0…80, Δτ=0.0001, 800,001 samples. Parameters: R₀=10 μm, ρ=998 kg/m³, μ=0.001 Pa·s, surface tension 0.0725 N/m, κ=4/3, ambient pressure 100,000 Pa, and vapor pressure 2,330 Pa. One τ equals 3.0123833 μs. This is the archived air-in-water system, not the earlier zero-vapor-pressure PDF example.

Joint training channels are radius and the registered far-field acoustic echo. Velocity is fitted as an additional output through the standard least-squares reconstruction method; the stored vector state map reconstructs both radius and velocity. Radius and echo also have separate direct Koopman-mode maps. This distinction matters: radius/echo maps come directly from Hankel DMD, while the vector Cx used for KLQE is the additional state-output fit.

HX[(d,c),j] = yc((d+245j)Δτ),
HY[(d,c),j] = yc((d+245j+1)Δτ),
d=0,…,12750;   c ∈ {radius, echo};   j=0,…,3199.

Thus each matrix is 25,502×3,200. The history spans 1.275 τ. Window starts are separated by 245 samples to cover the ring-down economically, but the fitted operator advances one sample, Δτ=0.0001. This is not an operator with step 0.0245. Channels have scale one and are not centered. Changing channel scaling changes the fitting objective and is a legitimate future sensitivity test.

HX ≈ UΣVᵀ,   Ã = UᵀHYVΣ−1,
ÃW = W diag(μ),   modes = UW,   Ψ ≈ W+UᵀH.
λⱼ = log(μⱼ)/Δτ,   Cyᵢ = the channel-i row of UW at the selected window endpoint.

Endpoint alignment is causal: each reference Ψ is labeled by the newest measurement in its window. The stored fitting times start at τ=1.275 and end at 79.6506. Consecutive archived coordinates are usually separated by 0.0245, with one final adjacent pair separated by 0.0001. The reported propagation errors primarily test the 0.0245 interval; they are not a full paired one-step residual test.

“Reference Ψ” here means coordinates projected from the joint training windows. They are not known exact eigenfunctions. During online KLQE, neither radius nor velocity nor joint windows are supplied to the observer. Only the scalar echo is supplied. Physical states are used offline to generate synthetic measurements and evaluate errors. Held-out deployment does not use state-space interpolation to initialize Ψ.

3. The 3,188-mode bank: excellent snapshot reconstruction, poor modal reliability

The requested cap was 3,200; an absolute singular-value cutoff of 10⁻¹⁰ retained 3,188. The first singular value is 7,949.56; the last of all 3,200 is 8.87×10⁻¹¹. A machine-scale diagnostic ε·max(rows,columns)·σ₁ is approximately 4.5×10⁻⁸. Many retained directions lie below that scale. Retaining more than 3,000 coordinates therefore does not demonstrate more than 3,000 reliable dynamical eigenfunctions.

Using all reference Ψ coordinates, fitted-sample RMSE is 3.75×10⁻⁸ for r, 2.38×10⁻⁵ for v, and 3.30×10⁻⁸ for echo. Yet 63 modes have positive real rate above 10⁻⁶, and the largest rate is about +508.27. Strong decoder columns have normalized contributions of order millions that cancel. For example, mode 3090 has λ≈−0.1996+6.6273i, reconstruction importance ≈2.06×10⁶, and normalized adjacent-stored-sample propagation defect ≈1.431. Snapshot fit alone is misleading.

The full complex modal Gramian above uses horizon 0.001 because unstable fitted modes overflow at long horizons. It has numerical rank 4 under the recorded RMS scaling and threshold. This is a short-horizon numerical diagnostic, not proof that the physical system has only four observable degrees of freedom, and not the subset-selection horizon. Conjugate constraints also mean that the full complex bank is not a bank of independent real coordinates.

The full-bank plot uses the archived fitted endpoints, which undersample the sharpest collapse peaks compared with the full-rate observer plots below. All RMSE values use the stated native arrays at their actual sampling rates; no interpolation or plot decimation enters the error computation.

4. Candidate subsets and what the search found

The search keeps conjugate pairs together, checks the sampled conjugacy relation, RMS normalizes, and considers rates with real part between −50 and 0.01 and |imaginary part|≤1,000. A per-mode normalized propagation RMS below 0.5 admits 43 representatives. This generous screening limit is an experimental choice, not a theorem or application-quality threshold.

Candidate ordering uses normalized state contribution; echo visibility; or a heuristic product importance×visibility/(1+visibility)/(1+defect). Prefix sizes 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32 are assessed as complete subsets. Duplicate sets are removed, leaving 15 candidates. Each uses an observability horizon T=2 and sample interval 0.0001, with 20,000 hypothetical independent noisy measurements.

A numerical-real check was added and the screening repeated: rates with tiny imaginary parts (relative tolerance 10⁻⁷) may be snapped to real only if their sampled coordinates also pass the real-up-to-phase check. The nominally near-zero mode was then considered but had propagation defect ≈1.567 and was still rejected. The set of eligible modes and candidate scores did not change. The failure is not explained solely by that numerical classification edge case.

No acceptable subset was found in this bounded search. The best score is about 8.706, including normalized state truncation MSE 7.618 and relative echo MSE about 1.001. Its noise benchmark is small because its retained state contributions are tiny; the large truncation penalty correctly exposes the failure. This is not an exhaustive search over all 3,188-mode subsets and does not prove that no better subset exists.

5. Actual KLQE experiments

dẑ/dτ = Aẑ + L(y − Cyẑ),   x̂ = Cxẑ.
AP + PAᵀ − PCyᵀR−1CyP + Q = 0,   L = PCyᵀR−1.

The standard native continuous KLQE design and runner are used. Q=10⁻⁴I in normalized independent real coordinates. Initial estimated coordinates are zero. The runner uses exact linear propagation with held measurements at Δτ=0.0001; it caches the equal-step propagator. For clean measurements the design covariance floor is R=10⁻¹⁰. For the noisy run, independent Gaussian noise has σ=0.0382082, seed 20261105, and R=σ²Δτ. This continuous-noise scaling is a design convention, not a claim of an exactly equivalent discrete Kalman filter.

Separate conditioning comparisons: 600 and 80 modes

Both comparison fits use exactly the same source, Hankel geometry, channels, scaling, and time step; only the rank cap changes. They are new fits, not subsets of the 3,188 bank. The 600-mode bank gives snapshot RMSE approximately (2.78×10⁻⁶, 0.001128) in (r,v), but still has 66 growing modes and severe cancellation. Its tested screened subsets are worse by the selection score.

The 80-mode bank has one growing mode, full-reference reconstruction RMSE approximately (0.09356, 1.20825), and echo RMSE 1.71747. None of its oscillatory representatives passes the original propagation screen. To test whether that screen was hiding a workable observer, all 80 modes were retained as 41 representatives, including two numerically real coordinates, with no C refit. Its whole-bank assessment is: state truncation MSE 0.254581, relative echo MSE 0.200825, dynamics MSE 1.63296, state-noise benchmark 3.15775, and numerical observability rank 13 of 80 at T=2.

Clean echo matching succeeds much better than state estimation. Late-time echo RMSE is 3.82×10⁻⁵, but radius RMSE remains 0.99761. This is an explicit demonstration that echo agreement does not establish accurate Ψ or accurate x. The fitted modal dynamics and decoder are not jointly reliable enough for that inference.

Separate the baseline failure from oscillatory error. The radius error near one is dominated by a missing equilibrium contribution. As a post-hoc arithmetic diagnostic only, adding a known offset of one to the 80-mode radius estimate would reduce full-interval radius RMSE to 0.29895 and late-time radius RMSE to 0.01143. It does not change velocity: late-time velocity RMSE is still 0.09095 against a true velocity RMS of 0.13152 (about 69%). On the mild held-out trajectory, the same hypothetical radius offset reduces full-interval radius RMSE to 0.01430, while velocity RMSE remains 0.1100 versus true RMS 0.1613. No offset was applied to the observer outputs or plots. Thus the absolute-radius failure should not be mistaken for complete absence of dynamical information, but correcting that baseline alone does not produce an accurate state estimator.

Noise and a new trajectory

6. What is standing in the way, and the next experiments I recommend

  1. Protect the equilibrium contribution explicitly. A constant eigenfunction ψ₀=1 with μ₀=1 (λ₀=0), or a declared equilibrium offset, is physically appropriate for absolute radius. The present unconstrained fits do not provide a reliable dynamically constant radius baseline. This should be checked before any subset ranking. Do not silently add one to the plotted estimates; it changes the model and belongs in a separate recorded fit.
  2. Treat a known constant as known. Hold ψ₀=1 fixed or use an explicit reconstruction offset; do not ask the filter to infer that coordinate as an unknown. If its echo coefficient is zero, an unknown constant mode is unobservable and marginal, which can break detectability and Riccati design. Selection should distinguish known offsets from uncertain dynamical coordinates.
  3. Choose the fitting rank by predictive validation, not by a minimum desired mode count. The 3,188 bank includes nearly null singular directions. The 600- and 80-mode comparisons show that truncation alone is not sufficient. Test rank, history length, and channel scaling together, using paired-step defects and longer rollouts over early, middle, and late decay. Strongly nonlinear, amplitude-dependent ring-down is difficult for one finite linear model.
  4. Use independent unforced trajectories for selection and assessment. The present subset scores use fitted samples and therefore remain optimistic. Multi-trajectory fitting and held-out reconstruction/propagation are needed before calling any retained coordinate a reliable eigenfunction over a region.
  5. Use reconstruction plus state-weighted observability as a Pareto problem, with a dynamics-quality gate. Set physical accuracy limits for radius and velocity; specify sensor noise/bandwidth and a meaningful convergence horizon; reject high truncation error; then compare the remaining uncertainty/dynamics/size tradeoffs. Compare nested candidate groups and local add/remove swaps. Individual C norms or singular values alone cannot select a trustworthy subset.
  6. Require actual observer validation after ranking. Check state, echo, innovation, and reference-Ψ error separately under noise, initial uncertainty, and held-out ICs. Good echo fit is necessary but insufficient. Model mismatch and unmodeled contributions should enter the estimator covariance design; increasing gain merely to match echo can mask them.

The experiment identifies a failure of these particular fitted models and screened subsets. It does not establish that echo-only state estimation is impossible, that the underlying RPE system is unobservable, or that all joint Hankel models must fail. Nor does it establish the global optimum over subsets. A robust subset should not be certified until the model itself passes the dynamical and reconstruction checks.

7. What is now in the standard pipeline

  • Spectral.hpp: joint scalar-channel Hankel DMD, explicit channel scales, causal endpoint selection, separate window-start stride and operator step, and named reconstruction rows. Existing scalar overloads remain available.
  • Reconstruction.hpp: fit and attach any named observable reconstruction from aligned Ψ/output samples, with optional ridge. No equations of motion are needed for the measured-data overload. The compiled observable_reconstruction stage writes a new immutable KROM.
  • Observability.hpp, Controllability.hpp, Pbh.hpp: matrix and KROM analyses, continuous/discrete finite and infinite Gramians, classical matrices/SVDs, strongest/weakest directions, numerical rank/conditioning, and PBH detectability/stabilizability margins. Infinite Gramians require strict stability. Nonconstant control maps require a stated local operating-point matrix.
  • ModalObservability.hpp: analytic diagonal-modal Gramian assembly for large banks, followed by Hermitian spectral decomposition.
  • EstimationRealization.hpp and EstimationSelection.hpp: verified conjugate reduction, normalization, and whole-subset assessment. These produce/use ordinary KROMs and existing named C maps.
  • The native post-processing UI accepts multiple comma-separated observables, window alignment/stride, and additional reconstruction outputs. The compiled linear_analysis stage exposes diagnostics independently of any observer design.
